# Worldline interaction and adaptive research

“Worldlines” are conditional candidate scenarios, not proven causal timelines. “Broad research” means
investigating valuable gaps, not exhausting the Web. Product direction is [goal.md](../goal.md).

## Interaction direction

Present summary first, then comparable candidate cards with mechanisms, conditions, tradeoffs and
revision ancestry. Keep public evidence separate from the execution DAG; neither is the scenario's
chronological timeline. Graphs and accessible lists share one selected issue. Keyboard activation,
filtering and focus must update the same details as mouse selection; filtering a selected issue clears
stale details without remounting the viewport. Embedded canvases must not steal page scrolling.

The next UI increment maps each claim to its own citations and offers a return-to-node action. Task-level
references remain task-level; title similarity is not claim identity. Missing actors, signals or
objections are shown as unavailable, not inferred.

## Why constants are not architecture

The current v2 evidence bundle is bounded inside a run snapshot. More pages alone cannot provide
coverage: early-passage bias and the absence of a persistent research frontier are separate problems.
The collector already rotates attempts across queries; the next design must preserve that fairness
while moving source versions, passages and research queues into authenticated paginated storage.
Preserve old snapshot IDs, exact spans, hashes, task-visible validation and prompt limits.

The frontier should record questions, goal facets, priorities, attempts and evidence. Alternate search,
reading and gap assessment; deduplicate final URLs and near-duplicate source families; distinguish
credibility ranking from verification. Stop with a reason and remaining gaps. Never show a universal
completeness percentage.

## Acceptance

Before calling research expandable: a later passage can be selected; another query and source are
handled fairly; critique schedules an actual follow-up; reposts are not counted as independent support;
empty plans, failures and no-new-evidence stop honestly; cancellation/recovery preserve budgets and do
not duplicate evidence; evidence beyond the old snapshot is paginated; old projects remain readable.
Run real online/model checks—fixtures establish contracts, not research quality.
